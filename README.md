# My Favorite Movie

**Your Name** is a Japanese animated romantic fantasy movie directed by **Makoto Shinkai**. It was released in 2016.

The story follows two teenagers, **Taki Tachibana** and **Mitsuha Miyamizu**, who mysteriously switch bodies even though they live far away from each other. As they try to understand what is happening, they discover a special connection that goes beyond time and distance.

## Why I Love This Movie

- Beautiful animation and scenery.
- Emotional and meaningful storyline.
- Amazing music and soundtrack.
- Interesting and lovable characters.
- It teaches the importance of love, memories, and destiny.

### I love this movie because it shows that true connections can remain strong despite distance and time. It is a beautiful and unforgettable film.
